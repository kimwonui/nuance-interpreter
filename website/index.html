Hear the meaning

Not just the words.
The way you said them.

Word-for-word translation loses what people actually mean. NUA is built around a different goal: carry the intent and the feeling across, in a voice that keeps your pace.

Meaning over literal

Aims to say what you meant, the way a person would say it in the other language.

Feeling and tone

Warm should stay warm, a joke should stay light, and urgency should still sound urgent. That's the target.

Your pace

Designed to follow how fast or slowly you speak, instead of one flat reading speed.

Rhythm and pauses

Waits for the speaker to finish before answering, then speaks with natural pauses of its own.

This is the direction NUA is built toward, not a promise. Results vary by language, voice, and setting, and the prototype is still being tested.

    FAQ

    Good to know

    Can I download NUA?

    Not yet. NUA is an iPhone prototype that runs as a development build. It isn't on the App Store, and there's no sign-up or waitlist on this page.

    Does it need the internet?

    Yes. Interpretation runs on OpenAI's real-time voice models over the network, so NUA needs a connection to work.

    Do we press a button every time we talk?

    No. Tap the orb once to start. After that, each person speaks when they're ready and NUA interprets automatically when they pause. Mute turns the mic off; Stop ends the conversation.

    What's the difference between Two-way and One-way?

    Two-way interprets both people. One-way interprets only your language into theirs, for when you just need to be understood. One-way is new in the current build and hasn't been tested in real conversations yet.

    Where does the audio go?

    By default NUA uses GPT-Live: your speech and the spoken interpretation travel directly between the iPhone and OpenAI, which does the interpreting. The NUA server helps set up that connection and receives only numeric diagnostics such as timing, not what was said. The in-app History is kept in memory, not stored permanently. Audio handled by OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's own policies.

    How good is the interpretation?

    NUA is built to carry meaning, feeling, and pace in every language it offers. Like any interpreter, it can still make mistakes, and results depend on the speaker and the setting.
